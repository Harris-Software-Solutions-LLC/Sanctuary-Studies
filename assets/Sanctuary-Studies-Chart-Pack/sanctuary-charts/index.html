Reading the dates and locations

AD dates, Aaron’s symbolic service-day clock, and AM chronology are separate scales. AM means anno mundi, year of the world; 6,000 AM does not mean 6:00 a.m. Future AM positions are source chronology, not Gregorian predictions.

Steps 11–12 are entrance/court actions grouped along the Holy Place approach. Step 18 is at the entrance/courtyard boundary; Step 24 is outside the camp.

Step 5 is Daily; Steps 13–16 are Yearly. Step 14 has no Aaron counterpart. The interval between Steps 23–24 is not specified. The table of showbread has no numbered action in these diagrams.
