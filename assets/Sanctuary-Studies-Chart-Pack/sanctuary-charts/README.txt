SANCTUARY STUDIES CHART PACK

Open index.html in a browser or Electron window. Self-contained; no network or dependencies.
Five SVG charts and 2x PNG exports are in assets/. Embed SVG with <img src="assets/daily.svg" alt="Daily sanctuary timeline">; use width:100%; height:auto.
For responsive interactive integration, reuse the embedded renderer and sanctuary-steps.json. All views share one step dataset.
The viewer supports keyboard-accessible navigation, search, light/dark themes and print. No external fonts, analytics, or network requests.
Source grouping is preserved. Steps11–12 are approach/court actions, Step18 is a boundary action, Step24 outside camp. These distinctions are retained in notes.
AM is anno mundi, not a clock time. Future source chronology is not converted to Gregorian dates. No showbread step has been invented.
